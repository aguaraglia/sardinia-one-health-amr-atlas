Thirty short modules, ready for video or narrated presentations.

The pathway keeps the original twelve modules and adds eighteen on the burden of AMR, on mechanisms, and on molecular and environmental surveillance. Each record provides a learning objective, narrated scenes and verifiable sources. Local content remains distinct from surveillance data, environmental inference and health advice.
